Jenny Levack

Actor • Screen & Stage
Scottish classically trained actor for UK & European productions

I have worked across film, television and improv theatre since graduating from The Fontainebleau School of Acting (FONACT) in 2024. I work in English, Spanish, Portuguese and Catalan. Recent screen projects include The Ones On Top (dir. Joan Oliver), an upcoming feature with Jordi Mendives, an appearance in a popular Spanish television series, and I have just booked the supporting lead in a major feature film with an internationally acclaimed director.
