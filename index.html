Jenny Levack

Actor • Screen & Stage
Scottish classically trained actor for UK and European productions

I'm a Scottish, Barcelona-based multilingual actor working across film, television, and theatre. I graduated from FONACT in 2024 on the Two-Year Full-Time Acting Programme. Recent screen work includes a supporting role in the upcoming Netflix feature O Diário de Um Mago, El Caso Vitruvio, The Ones On Top, and an appearance in Com Si Fos Ahir. I work in English, Spanish, and Catalan, and I also speak Portuguese (I have a First Class Honours Degree in Hispanic Studies from The University of Glasgow). I do a lot of improv theatre and I'm currently exploring stand up. Oh! And I love tango!
